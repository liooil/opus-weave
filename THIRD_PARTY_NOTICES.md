## Bundled SoundFont

OpusWeave bundles `src/web/assets/opusweave-micro-gm.sf2`, a compact General
MIDI-compatible sound bank generated by the project from original synthetic
waveforms. It contains no third-party samples or recordings and is distributed
under the same Apache-2.0 license as OpusWeave. Users may still load their own
.sf2 / .sf3 / .sfogg files, subject to those files' respective licenses.
